1. Go to pexels.com or unsplash.com (both free to use, no sign-up needed to download).
2. Search: "luxury car parking garage"  (a dark or night photo suits the black theme).
3. Pick a WIDE (landscape) photo and download the medium / 1920 px size.
4. If the file is bigger than about 500 KB, shrink it at squoosh.app (free).
5. Rename it to hero.jpg and put it in this folder (static/img/).
6. Refresh the home page (Ctrl + F5).

Until you add it, the band shows a dark car-park pattern instead.
